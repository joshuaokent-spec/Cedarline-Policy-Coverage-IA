# Reference Content Inventory

This inventory is a **portfolio IA exercise**, not a claim that Cedarline or any real insurer uses this exact structure.

Public insurance sites commonly expose product pages, coverage subpages, claims resources, online-account functions, billing tasks and educational material. The inventory below captures the representative content types that the redesign must organize.

## IA issues the prototype is designed to address

1. Long product lists create scanning burden.
2. Service tasks can become buried under account or company-oriented labels.
3. Coverage questions often begin in plain language rather than product vocabulary.
4. Claims, billing and policy-management tasks have different mental models from product shopping.
5. Educational content can become detached from the policy or task that gives it context.
6. Similar information may need multiple contextual entry points without duplicating the underlying source of truth.
