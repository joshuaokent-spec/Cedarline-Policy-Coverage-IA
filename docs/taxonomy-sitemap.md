# Proposed Taxonomy & Sitemap

## Top-level navigation

### Insurance

Organized by the object or life area being protected:

- Vehicles
- Home & property
- Family & future
- Business & farm

This keeps formal product names available while reducing the first-level choice set.

### Manage my policy

For existing-customer service tasks:

- Billing & payments
- Documents & ID cards
- Policy changes
- Online account
- Road services

### Claims & help

For loss-related tasks and urgent support:

- Report a claim
- Track a claim
- Claim documents
- What happens next
- Storm / emergency resources
- Claim contact information

### Learn

For educational content:

- Coverage guides
- Insurance basics
- Discounts & savings
- FAQs
- Glossary

## Persistent actions

- Find an agent
- Sign in

These remain visible because they cut across the taxonomy.

## Coverage-page hierarchy

Coverage pages use a question-oriented hierarchy underneath the formal product name.

Example: **Homeowners**

1. Your home
2. Your belongings
3. Liability to others
4. Living somewhere else after a covered loss
5. Optional protection
6. Common situations
7. Policy-specific disclaimer / agent path

## Synonym strategy

Search and contextual linking should recognize user language such as:

- burst pipe → water damage / homeowners
- jewelry → valuables / personal property
- dog bite → liability
- insurance card → proof of insurance
- tow → roadside assistance
- apartment → renters
- landlord → rental dwelling
- lawsuit → liability / umbrella

The goal is not to replace formal terminology, but to provide a bridge to it.
